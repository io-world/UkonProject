# The assignment — UKON final round

Source: email from Matt Hightower (CEO, UKON). UKON states nothing in it is confidential UKON data.
Paid project ($200), "a few hours." AI tools are encouraged; say how you used them.

## Project: Cyber in the renewal window

**The scene.** UKON helps independent insurance agencies sell cyber insurance to their small and
mid-sized business clients. Most of those businesses don't have cyber, and most agents don't
bring it up. The best moment is the **renewal window**: the weeks before a client's existing
business policies renew, when the agent is already talking to them about coverage. Today that
moment is usually missed because getting a client from "no cyber" to "protected" is a lot of
manual work.

**People involved**
- **Agency staff:** producers and account managers who own the client relationship
- **Business owners:** the end customer; usually not tech savvy; don't really understand cyber
- **UKON's team:** Cyber Practice Leaders (CPLs) who support agencies, plus wholesale and operations
- **Carriers:** the insurance companies that quote and write the policies

**Goal:** get many more of an agency's clients quoted for cyber, and bound, during their renewal
window, with as little extra work for the agent as possible.

## How a client gets cyber today

1. **Get indications from carriers.** An indication is a non-binding ballpark premium for a given
   limit and deductible. Needs five fields: business legal name, website/domain, industry,
   annual revenue, state/business address.
2. **Build the client proposal pack.** For a non-technical owner: why cyber matters to *their*
   business, what the options are, what it costs, in plain language.
3. **Equip the agent.** Client-facing materials, a write-up on how to present (talking points,
   likely questions and objections), and the actual policy specimens.
4. **Present, apply, bind.** Agent presents; if the client wants to proceed there's an
   application (detailed business and security questions), the carrier confirms final terms,
   and the policy is bound.

## What they asked for

1. **Interview plan:** who you'd talk to (UKON execs and employees, agency users, business owners,
   carriers, others), why, and the 3–5 questions per group you'd most want answered before building.
2. **Workflow design:** end-to-end, from spotting an upcoming renewal to a bound policy. Per step:
   where AI is used and what it does, where a human stays in the loop and why, what could go
   wrong and how you'd catch it.
3. **Client deliverable:** what a non-technical owner wants to see in the proposal pack, and why.
4. **Prototype of one key step,** any tool; say what you used and why.
5. **Write-up (1–2 pages):** key assumptions, how you'd measure success, biggest risks (AI accuracy,
   compliance, data privacy, adoption), what you'd build first and deliberately skip, open questions.

**Deliverables:** interview plan · one-page workflow map · proposal pack outline or mockup ·
prototype link/screenshots/recording · short write-up.
