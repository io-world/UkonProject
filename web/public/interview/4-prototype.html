Prototype

A working local app, built around Stage 1 and carried through the later stages for one real client: Sloan's Lake Dental (legal name Sage Dentistry, PLLC), a three-dentist implant and cosmetic practice in Denver, Colorado. Everything public about it is real; the agency's records, the renewal date (January 15, 2027), the calls and the carriers are simulated. Screenshots are below; I'll demo it live.

Why Stage 1

Getting indications is the gate. If an agent has to hand-collect five fields for every renewing client, nothing downstream happens. It's also where AI judgment shows most clearly: knowing which estimates to trust, which to flag, and which always need a person. The other stages are built so the panel can see where Stage 1's output goes.

Which company is this?

The state registry has two entities at the practice's address. The one with the practice's own name has been delinquent since 2022; the one in good standing is Sage Dentistry, PLLC. Status counts, and a person still confirms.

Review by exception

Of the eight things Stage 1 produces, four need a person: nothing public gives this practice's revenue or headcount. The rest pass on agreement between independent public sources.

Revenue: two methods, one range

Nothing at the agency and nothing public, so revenue is estimated two ways. They disagree by almost $1.1M, so confidence is low and the range is wide. On the call the owner says it's higher and defers to the accountant: it stays flagged.

Exposure falls as controls are confirmed

A rough, explainable score (not a security rating). It starts from real public signals (patient data, online payments, no DMARC record) and drops as the calls confirm controls like MFA and endpoint detection.

Known gaps

Said up front, before they're asked.

  • The limit walkthrough isn't modelled yet. The formulas are written (lost income, notification cost and so on), but most of the figures show as placeholders until the defaults are sourced.
  • Without an API key, extraction, narrative and "Ask" use cached or offline outputs, checked by the same guardrails.
  • One client only. No renewal queue, sign-in, multi-agency separation, carrier integrations or e-signature.
  • Wispr Flow and phone calls: unverified.
  • Revenue per employee is a 2022 figure, not adjusted for inflation (conservative, and stated).
