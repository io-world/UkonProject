# Staff wages — Offices of Dentists (for the staff-cost and payroll figures)

Industry-keyed: one page per industry (`wages_<naics>.md`). The calculations in `pipeline/staff_costs.py` are the same for
every industry; only this page changes. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage
Statistics, May 2023, industry 621200 (Offices of Dentists) — https://www.bls.gov/OES/Current/naics4_621200.htm
(excludes the self-employed).

| Occupation | Employment | Mean annual wage |
|---|---|---|
| All occupations | 1,022,780 | $75,660 |
| Dentists (general + four specialist groups) | 122,760 | $194,400–$351,320 |
| Dental hygienists | 200,860 | $90,160 |
| Dental assistants | 339,100 | $47,280 |
| Receptionists and information clerks | 83,950 | $42,460 |
| Medical secretaries and administrative assistants | 91,570 | $46,500 |

**Default average staff pay: $58,155** = (all occupations' total wages − dentists' total wages) ÷ (all employment −
dentists' employment). Dentists are left out, the same as dental overhead leaves out the dentists' own pay; the
practice's headcount field is support staff only. Slider range: dental assistants ($47,280) to hygienists ($90,160).

## Limits

- Wages only: benefits and payroll taxes are not included, so real staff cost is higher.
- National means, not Denver and not this practice's payroll. The owner's real payroll replaces it once confirmed.
